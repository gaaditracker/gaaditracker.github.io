The driver app for Gaadi Tracker. Your owner creates your login; you record your shifts here.

START AND END YOUR SHIFT
• Enter the odometer reading to start. Enter it again to end.
• The app checks each reading against the vehicle's last one, so mistakes are caught on the spot.

ADD WHAT HAPPENED
• Earnings by platform, such as Uber, Ola, Rapido or local trips.
• Lorry and truck loads as tons × rate per ton. The app does the multiplication.

FORGOT TO START A SHIFT?
• Add a past trip later, up to one year back. It goes to your owner for approval.

SEE YOUR BALANCE
• "Balance to pay owner" or "Owner owes you", always up to date.

REMINDERS, NOT ROADBLOCKS

WORKS WITHOUT NETWORK
• Everything saves on your phone and syncs when the signal comes back. Highways, ghat roads, basement parking: your entries are safe.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• English, Tamil, Hindi, Tanglish or Hinglish. Pick the one you read best.

PRIVATE TO YOU
• You see only your own shifts, entries and balance.
